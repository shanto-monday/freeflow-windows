FreeFlow for Windows - talk, and clean text is typed where your cursor is.

1. Unzip, then double-click FreeFlow.exe.
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info" > "Run anyway".
   (The app is not signed with a paid certificate, that is why.)

2. Settings open. Pick how speech becomes text:
   - Cloud: get a free Groq key at https://console.groq.com/keys
     (sign up, "Create API Key", copy it) and paste it in the key box.
   - My GPU: runs on your own graphics card. One download (1.2 GB with NVIDIA,
     200 MB without). Works offline. A Groq key is still needed for clean up.

3. Click into any text box (email, chat, Word...).
   Hold Right Ctrl, talk, let go. Your words are pasted, cleaned up.

Talk key options (click the tray icon, bottom right, near the clock):
- Shortcut: click the key button, press any key or combo you like.
- Hold to talk: hold the key while you talk. Double-tap for hands-free.
- Tap to start / stop: tap once to start, tap again to stop.
- Esc cancels.

FreeFlow updates itself. Tray menu > Check for updates does it right away.
Needs Windows 10 or 11 and a microphone.
Tip: put FreeFlow.exe in a fixed folder (e.g. Documents) before turning on "Start with Windows".
